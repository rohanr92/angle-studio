01

Angle references

Upload one example photo per angle you want. Each is described in words (camera angle and composition only) and the photo itself is never sent, so nothing from it can leak into your images.

02

Reference product

Up to 6 photos of the actual product, different angles if possible. All of them are sent with every frame so the model learns the product properly.

Brand logo (optional). If you add one it is reproduced exactly; if you leave it empty, no logo or text is drawn at all.

Heel reference (optional). A photo showing only the heel / back shape of your product — used to get the heel height and profile right (e.g. flat jelly heel). Not treated as a product photo.

03

Brief (optional)

Optional product facts only (material, colour, construction). Leave empty if the photos say it all. Never describe background or lighting here.
